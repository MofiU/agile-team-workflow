# Scrum Guide Reference (2025 Edition)

This skill provides the authoritative reference for Scrum practices based on the 2025 Scrum Guide.

## Scrum Foundations

Scrum is founded on empiricism and lean thinking. It emphasizes:
- **Transparency**: Everyone shares a common understanding
- **Inspection**: Regularly examine Scrum artifacts and progress
- **Adaptation**: Adjust processes when issues are identified

## Three Pillars of Scrum

1. **Transparency**: Shared understanding of work across all involved
2. **Inspection**: Regularly review artifacts for quality and progress
3. **Adaptation**: Adjust processes when inspection reveals issues

## Five Scrum Events

### 1. The Sprint
- Fixed-length container (1-4 weeks) for all other events
- Previous Sprint ends when new Sprint begins
- No changes that endanger Sprint Goal
- Cancel a Sprint only if Sprint Goal becomes obsolete

### 2. Sprint Planning
- Time-box: 2 hours per week of Sprint (max 8 hours for 2-week Sprint)
- Answers: What can be done? How will it be achieved?
- Inputs: Product Backlog, Product Goal, team capacity
- Outputs: Sprint Goal, Sprint Backlog

### 3. Daily Scrum
- Time-box: 15 minutes
- Focus: Progress toward Sprint Goal
- Inspect: Yesterday, Today, Blockers
- Adapt Sprint Backlog as needed

### 4. Sprint Review
- Time-box: 4 hours for 2-week Sprint
- Inspect: Sprint outcomes
- Collaborate: What next? (adapt Product Backlog)
- Demonstrate: Completed work to stakeholders

### 5. Sprint Retrospective
- Time-box: 3 hours for 2-week Sprint
- Inspect: Individuals, interactions, tools, Definition of Done
- Plan: Improvements for next Sprint
- Create actionable improvement backlog

## Three Accountabilities

### Product Owner
- **Accountable for**: Maximizing product value, Product Backlog management
- **Key activities**: Expressing items, ordering backlog, ensuring visibility
- **Authority**: Final decision on product features and priorities

### Scrum Master
- **Accountable for**: Establishing Scrum as described in Guide
- **Key activities**: Facilitating events, removing impediments, coaching
- **Serves**: Product Owner, Development Team, Organization

### Developers
- **Accountable for**: Creating a plan for Sprint (Sprint Backlog)
- **Key activities**: Instilling quality, progressing toward Sprint Goal
- **Characteristics**: Cross-functional, self-managing, no sub-teams

## Scrum Artifacts

### Product Backlog
- Ordered list of what is needed for the product
- Single accountability: Product Owner
- Includes: Features, functions, requirements, fixes, work items
- Never complete, evolves with product and market

### Sprint Backlog
- Product Backlog items selected for Sprint + plan for delivery
- Created at Sprint Planning
- Owned by Developers
- Can be clarified and re-ordered during Sprint

### Increment
- Sum of all completed Product Backlog items
- Must be usable and meet Definition of Done
- inspected at Sprint Review
- Released at Product Owner's discretion

## Definition of Done

Shared understanding of what it means for work to be complete. When applied, it increases transparency and quality.

## Key Principles

### Sprints
- All work occurs within a Sprint
- No hierarchical distinctions in Sprint
- Quality does not decrease with longer Sprints
- Scope may be clarified with Product Owner

### Commitment
- Product Owner commits to Product Goal
- Team commits to Sprint Goal
- Each item has a commitment (Sprint Backlog)
- Definition of Done is the commitment for Increment

### Self-Organization
- Teams choose how to accomplish work
- No externally-imposed approach
- More than one Scrum Team working on same product should share artifacts appropriately

## Event Timing Summary

| Event | Time-Box (2-week Sprint) |
|-------|-------------------------|
| Sprint Planning | 4 hours |
| Daily Scrum | 15 minutes daily |
| Sprint Review | 4 hours |
| Sprint Retrospective | 3 hours |

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Sprint Goal changes mid-Sprint
- Adding new items to Sprint Backlog mid-Sprint
- Skipping Daily Scrum
- Treating retrospective action items as optional
- Definition of Done applied inconsistently
- Product Owner unavailable for clarification
- Scrum Master as team lead/manager
- Separate "development team" and "testing team"

## AI as an Actor in Scrum

Per the Scrum Guide Expansion Pack (2025):
- AI agents can be recognized as team members
- AI assistance should support, not replace, human judgment
- Use AI to enhance productivity while maintaining accountability
- Human team members remain accountable for outcomes
